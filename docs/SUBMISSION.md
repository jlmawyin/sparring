## Posicionamiento

Título: Sparring — Practice tough customer conversations.

Descripción corta propuesta:

Sparring helps sales and support teams rehearse difficult customer conversations by voice, see evidence behind their feedback, and practice a better response before talking to real customers.

Descripción larga propuesta (futuro objetivo; convertir a presente sólo al comprobarlo):

Sales and support teams often practice difficult conversations with a colleague, or learn while dealing with real customers. Sparring is designed to provide short, repeatable voice practice with a simulated customer who challenges vague answers and raises realistic objections. The prototype focuses on three situations: a late delivery, a price comparison, and a cancellation request after an apparent duplicate charge. AssemblyAI powers the spoken interaction. During practice, function calls propose behavioral observations, and application logic validates quoted evidence and calculates a transparent rubric. After the conversation, the learner receives a focused improvement and a suggested response to practice again. Sparring targets small Spanish-speaking teams that need accessible practice without complex integrations. Scores are formative feedback, with explicit coverage and limitations, rather than professional certification. Our hackathon evaluation will report the sessions actually tested and the limitations observed.
