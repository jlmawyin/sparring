1. The Problem

Training for Tough Conversations is Hard

Sales and support teams—especially small ones—face a difficult challenge:

  • Role-playing with colleagues is awkward and doesn't scale.
  • Learning on the job with real customers risks relationships and revenue.
  • Generic scripts rarely reproduce the pressure and timing of a real voice call.

They need a lightweight, accessible way to practice difficult conversations before the real call.

2. The Product

Sparring: Practice by Voice

Sparring is designed to provide short, repeatable voice practice with a simulated customer.

  • Select: Choose a difficult scenario (e.g., late delivery, price comparison, cancellation request).
  • Practice: Talk to a simulated customer who challenges vague answers and raises realistic objections.
  • Review: Understand the evidence behind your feedback.
  • Repeat: Practice a better response to build confidence.

3. In Action

Demo Scenario: Late Delivery

Example scenario: a customer asks for a 30% discount after a delayed order. The trainee has limited authority and must offer a policy-safe next step.

During the call: a tool proposes an observation, names the rubric criterion, and supplies an exact trainee quote. The server checks that evidence before updating the score.

The session ends with targeted coaching: one strength, one area for improvement, and an alternative response that stays within company policy.

4. Technology

Built for Trust & Transparency

  • Voice interaction: The browser connects to AssemblyAI's Voice Agent API using a short-lived server-issued token.
  • Real-time Hooks: Function calls propose behavioral observations during the call.
  • Deterministic Validation: Application logic strictly validates quoted evidence and calculates a transparent rubric.

Trust Boundaries: Scores are formative feedback, not professional certification. Our tool shows the exact coverage and limitations of every AI judgment.

4 / 6

5. Business Value

Practice Before the Real Call

  • Buyer hypothesis: Small Spanish-speaking sales and support teams need repeatable practice without a complex rollout.
  • Distinctive loop: Short voice role-play, evidence-backed feedback, and immediate retry.
  • Pilot measure: Completion, perceived usefulness, and whether the next attempt improves one specific behavior.

Automated tests use a mock provider. Human voice testing and team pilot results will be reported separately.
